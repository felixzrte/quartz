# The Big Three
---
The most powerful corporate empires, the Big Three, have their headquarters in the [[The Used T'Be Forest]]: the ever-expanding [[Subterranean Pits and Lairs LLC]], the ruthless and greedy [[Krog & Sons Inc.]], and the mercilessly efficient [[Unassailable Co.]] All are eager to assert their dominance and widen their influence across the [[Mucklands]], caring little for the people of Eem.
