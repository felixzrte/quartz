# Mucklands Trading Co.
---
The powerful dungeoneer outfits of the [[The Used T'Be Forest]] knew that the only way to expand their interests beyond the [[Mucklands]] was to establish a strong arm for trading. And so together, the leaders of the [[Big Three]] pooled their resources to create an independent entity to preserve and protect their investments on equal ground. The resulting Mucklands Trading Co. quickly
emerged as an influential force in the realm. Sporting thousands of agents and a Navy to rival
the galleons of [[Felmog]], the Mucklands Trading Co. has ensured safe transport, trade, banking, and warehousing for the powers of the [[Dungeon Era]] for fifty years.
