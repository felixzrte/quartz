**_Hex 22.23._** A bustling trading town, and the most trafficked population center in [[Scalawag Strand]]. The dungeoneering outfits of the [[Mucklands]] transport massive amounts of resources, materials, goods, and personnel from Muckland Harbor to fuel their mighty industrial economy throughout all of Eem. Ships dock and set sail across the seas daily, bursting with wares to fuel the growth of the [[Dungeon Era]]. But with wealth comes danger, and Muckland Harbor has long been harried by pirates and freebooters.
