# Slick Eddie and His Crew
---
## Overview

A crew led by [["Slick" Eddie]] has been running rigged gambling games on the docks for months, scamming honest folks out of their money. Most victims walk away thinking they're just unlucky, but [[G. Leonard Thistlebottom|Leonard]] caught them cheating red-handed (at the cost of a sore nose). [[Gilly Blubwith]] has been waiting for someone with actual proof of their operations and is ready to take action against them.
