## Personality

### Ideals

Risk my life to save an animal or to preserve nature.

### Flaws

Trust in someone dubious.

## Backstory

Left [[The Dingledell]] to combat the destruction of nature happening in the [[Mucklands]]. Spent most of his time replanting trees in the [[The Used T'Be Forest]], until he was tricked by a [[Kobold]] named [[Sheisty]] who stole Leonards bag of seeds. It was Leonards most precious possession given to him by his mentor [[B. Gibbly Bernhard]].

## Personal Quest

More than anything, Leonard desires to restore the [[Used T'Be Forest]] to its former glory. The stolen seeds have been a massive setback, but it has not deterred Leonard from his quest.

## Relationships

- [[Delmer]] : Leonard understands [[Delmer|Delmer's]] desire to find the wizard as he too has a mentor who means a lot to him. I think in many ways Leonard relates with [[Delmer]]. Leonard enjoys cooking and sharing knowledge back and forth with [[Delmer]].
- [[Hrg]] : While his love for nature is specific, Leonard appreciates [[Hrg|Hrg's]] fixation on knowing/catching/discovering the fish of the [[Mucklands]]. Leonard believes that the more you know and love nature the more you care about conservation. He hopes this quest for [[Hrg]] will have that impact.
- [[Cael Crushclaw|Big Blue]] : In their short friendship [[Cael Crushclaw|Blue]] has rescued Leonard more than once at this point. Leonard is a dangerous combination of being brave and willing to put himself in danger... but naive, particularly when it comes to other folks. [[Cael Crushclaw|Blue]] respects Leonards courage but also sees that Leonard is not very discerning. [[Cael Crushclaw|Blue]] on the other-hand can sniff out a sketchy situation from a mile away.
- [[Calcium Carpal|Carpal]] : Leonard completely trusts [[Calcium Carpal|Carpal]] and is fascinated by him. He has never seen a talking skeleton before, and [[Calcium Carpal|Carpal]] is capable of [[Dwimmercrafty]]. While Leonard trusts competely (sometimes to a point of being foolish), abuse of [[Dwimmercrafty]] or misuse will put a roadblock in what is currently a blossoming friendship.
