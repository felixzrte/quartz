## Backstory
Left [[The Dingledell]] to combat the destruction of nature happening in the [[Mucklands]]. Spent most of his time replanting trees in the [[Used T’be Forest]], until he was tricked by a [[Kobold]] named [[Sheisty]] who stole Leonards bag of seeds. It was Leonards most precious possession given to him by his mentor [[B. Gibbly Bernhard]]. 
## Personal Quest
More than anything, Leonard desires to restore the [[Used T’be Forest]] to its former glory. The stolen seeds have been a massive setback, but it has not deterred Leonard from his quest.
