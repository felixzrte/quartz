# 1.  Introduction
Hey yall, my name is Chuyo. I enjoy worldbuilding and my goal is always that my players feel like they can make an impact on the world I present them. I am still new to TTRPGs but it is a hobby that I really enjoy.

When I came across Land Of Eem I was immediately intrigued and looked into the system more and more. I joined the discord and looked for any PBP games but alas there were none which wasn't surprising to me since I'd consider it a niche style. This is the reason why I decided to take upon the role of a GM and run a PBP game myself! Although I don't know what to expect, my ultimate goal is to just have fun. In the next couple sections I will talk about how I plan to run the game and what you'd expect as a player. 
# 2. What to Expect
Not going to lie, I don't even know what to expect but I do know the core things I want to do as a GM! Those being:
- Sandbox Experience
- A Living World
- Rich Storytelling
- A Collaborative Experience
## 2.1. Sandbox Experience
I am sure you've seen the Mucklands map by now and let me tell you that what they've done is a GMs dream! A beautiful map with so many (I mean like SO many) things to be discovered and to go along with that map there is also the campaign book! With these resources the sandbox experience that can be had is endless with so many possibilities and adventures to be had. 

Of course with so many choices it can also be daunting for a player cause there is just so much! And with this being a PBP game it will be slower pace meaning that to visit a new location might take a while. That's why I will try my best to keep the pace going and really streamline things and organize things the best I can.

My goal is for this world to be open for the players to explore and this will require motivation and initiative from the party. Of course I will present interesting hooks, rumors and the such but it comes down to if the players want to interact with the world or not. 
## 2.2. A Living World
As of now I will be the only GM so I don't plan any grandiose experience but what I want to say is that I don't want the people in this world to feel bland. I don't want to present an NPC to the players that just gives them a hook I want to follow and that's it. I want the NPCs to feel real and they have their motivations as well, they won't always want what the players want. I want these NPCs to have connections with other NPCs, factions, etc. so that overtime the web of connections begin to grow and the players can now come up with some pretty interesting ideas and I as the GM will just go along with it, cause this is the experience I want to create; the players interacting with the world I present. 

The same thing could be said about locations. Say the party visits a location they've been to before, who is to say it is the exact same as before? Or what if their favorite NPC no longer lives there? What if they went missing? These are things that I take into account as well in hope to craft that "Living World" experience.
## 2.3. Rich Storytelling
The great thing about PBP is exactly this. There is more time to craft responses and include details and be descriptive and that is the experience I would like to create. I am no writer, far from it. I am not looking for professional writing but each character has there own quirks and acts differently so put that into your writing and feel free to be descriptive! No 10 paragraphs but also no one liners (depending on the context).

It is important to read others peoples posts and if there is something to be clarified then that can be done in the out of character chat unless you think your character actually wouldn't understand something another player posted. I want to ensure that we are all on the same page so that we can ensure that we all have the same context so that things just make sense. This also means that all players should have a chance to talk and that it is not just one person deciding and then just going forward in a scene which leads me to my final point.
## 2.4. A Collaborative Experience
Land of Eem starts with this from the beginning; you will have a connection of some sort with every player. That is to say that collaboration is something I will say is necessary to make this game fun and enjoyable for everyone. There are also mechanics in this game in which collaboration is emphasized, for example the Bard has some abilities in which they can explain how something actually happened. I also want to say I respect suggestions (that is even a channel in the discord) and also even criticism (which is always given with respect). I say this cause I want to improve and if you have an idea or input I would like to hear it! My goal is to say at the end of the day "We created this world". Even if a player plays for 4 months or a year the impact of that player's actions will affect the world forever.
